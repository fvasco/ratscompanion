Privacy Policy for Rats Companion

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy covers Rats Companion on Android and in the browser. Francesco Vasco is the developer and the data controller for the processing he is responsible for under this policy. You can reach him at fsco_v-github@yahoo.it.

Neither version requires an account. The developer does not receive your game saves or keep a user database. The hosting and advertising providers described below may still process technical data, and the developer receives the information you choose to send by email.

Game saves and settings

Game data, including character sheets, notes, monsters and settings, is saved locally. Rats Companion does not sync saves between devices. You control the local copy:

  • Android: Saves are kept in the app's private storage. Android can include them in system backups or device transfers, depending on your device and settings. You can remove the local copy by clearing the app's storage in Android settings. Backups are managed separately through your device's backup settings.
  • Web: Saves are kept in your browser's local storage for this site and browser profile. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes those saves. The web app also caches its files for offline use after the first online load; that cache is separate from your saves.

Advertising and privacy choices on Android

The Android app uses Google AdMob to display a banner in the navigation drawer. Once there is at least one character, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether a privacy form is needed and shows it when required. The app requests ads only when UMP indicates that it can. When UMP makes privacy options available, open the top-right menu and select Privacy to review or change your choices.

Google may process technical data such as your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics and, where applicable, identifiers such as the app set ID. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, diagnose problems and prevent fraud. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children's privacy

The app does not ask for your age or date of birth. On Android, it applies the same protections to everyone: it tells UMP to treat users as under the age of consent and configures AdMob for child age treatment, with a maximum ad content rating of G (general audiences). For these ad requests, Google disables personalized ads and remarketing and does not transmit the Android advertising ID.

Web hosting, cookies and local storage

The web app does not include ads, UMP or analytics scripts, and its own code does not set cookies. It uses browser storage for the saves and offline files described above.

Cloudflare hosts the web app. When you visit it or download an update, Cloudflare may process your IP address and the URL requested to deliver and protect the site. It may also receive browser reports about network errors to diagnose connection problems. If Cloudflare presents a security challenge, it may set a security cookie. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and its cookie documentation.

This policy is hosted separately on GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs and stores the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security, even if they are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Where the GDPR applies, the developer relies on legitimate interests in making the app and its privacy policy available, keeping them secure and resolving technical problems. The providers' privacy notices describe their own retention practices and international transfers.

Email and your privacy rights

If you email the developer, he uses your email address and the information you supply to respond and handle any necessary follow-up. Under the GDPR, the basis is the legitimate interest in responding to your message, or compliance with a legal obligation if one applies. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up, unless the law requires a longer period.

Where the GDPR applies, you can request access, correction, erasure or restriction of personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and request data portability where applicable. You can withdraw consent for any processing based on consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact the developer at fsco_v-github@yahoo.it. The developer cannot retrieve or erase game saves for you; use the device or browser controls described above. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority, such as the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali in Italy.
