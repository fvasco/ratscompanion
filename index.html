What you need to play

Maze Rats is an old-school game with simple rules that are easy to teach and leave room for improvisation. Rats Companion is an unofficial tool, not a standalone game. You'll still need the rulebook and a group to play with.

Privacy at a glance

No account is required. Game saves are stored locally; Android may include them in system backups. The app uses non-personalized ads.
